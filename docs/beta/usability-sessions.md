# Usability sessions

Clearing collects no telemetry, so the PRD §11 targets are measured in
person. Five participants, about 30 minutes each, on their own phone if
possible (fresh install), or a test phone.

Say at the start: *"We're testing the app, not you. Think aloud. I won't help
unless you're stuck for a while."* Time with a stopwatch. Don't demo first.

## Tasks

| # | Task (read it out) | Measures | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Add the quick-capture widget to your home screen." (help is fine here) | n/a | n/a |
| 2 | "You just remembered you need to buy stamps. Get that into the app, starting from the home screen." | Time from widget tap to saved | **Median ≤ 5 s** |
| 3 | "Look at what you just saved and decide when you'll do it: this week." | Done without help? | **4 of 5 unaided** |
| 4 | "Make 'Pay rent' happen on the 1st of every month, with a reminder the day before." | Done without help? Errors | observe |
| 5 | Pre-load ~20 inbox items. "Do your weekly review." | Time to finish | **Median ≤ 15 min** |
| 6 | "You feel overwhelmed. Use the app to get down to one thing." | Time to focus view | **≤ 5 min** (FR-12) |
| 7 | "Mark today's must-do done from the home screen." | Works without opening the app? | observe |
| 8 | "Add *pay rent every 1st, remind me the day before*" by typing only. | Done without help? | **4 of 5 unaided** |
| 9 | Pre-load 5 inbox items. "Move these five to this week, at once." | Time | **≤ 30 s median** |
| 10 | "Find something you finished last week." | Done without help? | **4 of 5 unaided** |
| 11 | Pre-load items with lengths. "You have 15 minutes. Find something to do." | Time | **≤ 20 s median** |
| 12 | "Get *buy stamps on Friday* into Today from the home screen." | Time, extra taps after typing | **≤ 8 s median, none** |

## Afterwards

- Which words were confusing? (Clarify, Someday, Waiting, carried over,
  must-do…)
- Did anything feel like nagging or guilt?
- (If they turned on the Grove) How did the growing clearing feel?
  Encouraging, neutral, or pressure?
- Would you keep using it? Why or why not?

Record per participant: times, unaided yes/no, quotes, and bugs. Summarise
against the targets in `exit-checklist.md`.

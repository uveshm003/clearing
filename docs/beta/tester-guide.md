# Welcome to the Clearing beta

Thank you for trying Clearing. It's a small app for getting unfinished
thoughts out of your head and trusting they won't be forgotten. The beta runs
for **2–4 weeks**.

**Your data stays on your phone.** The app has no internet access. Before the
beta ends, use Settings › Backup to export a copy if you want to keep your
items.

## Week 1: just capture

- Add the **quick-capture widget** (long-press the home screen › Widgets ›
  Clearing). Whenever something pops into your head, put it in. One line is
  enough.
- Once a day, open the **Inbox** and decide what each one means.
- Pick up to three **must-dos** for Today.

## Week 2: let it remind you

- Give a few things a date and a reminder. Try **Snooze** and **Done** right
  from the notification.
- Set up something that repeats (rent, bins, meds).
- Do your first **weekly review** (Today › ⋮ › Weekly review). It should take
  about 15 minutes.

## Any time

- Feeling swamped? Try **Overwhelm reset** (Today › ⋮).
- Try the **Today widget** and tick things off from the home screen.

## Telling us what you think

Settings › About and feedback › **Send feedback**. It opens your email or a
messaging app with a short template; nothing is sent automatically. Bugs,
confusing words, anything that felt like nagging: all of it helps.

At the end you'll get a short survey (about 5 minutes).

**Reminders not arriving?** See Settings › Reminders not arriving? for your
phone's battery settings. Please tell us your phone model if it happens.

# Clearing privacy policy

_Last updated: <date of publishing>_

Clearing is a to-do and planning app that works entirely on your phone.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Clearing has no account, no server, no analytics, no crash
reporting and no ads. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot
send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you type into Clearing (items, notes, steps, lists, reminders and
settings) is stored in a database on your device only. It is not included in
Google's cloud backup. It does move to a new phone if you use Android's
phone-to-phone transfer.

## Backups you export

If you choose **Export a backup**, Clearing creates a file and hands it to the
app or folder you pick. Where that file goes is up to you. Clearing never
uploads it.

## Feedback

**Send feedback** opens an app of your choice (for example, email) with a short
template containing the app's version number. Nothing is sent unless you send
it yourself.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** and **exact alarms**: to show the reminders you set, at
  the time you set.
- **Run at startup**: to put reminders back after the phone restarts.

## Children

Clearing is not directed at children under 13.

## Full description

> Your brain wasn't built to hold forty open loops. Clearing is a calm place
> to put them, so you can stop rehearsing them.
>
> **Capture in one step.** A thought, a worry, a "don't forget": type one line
> and it's saved. Use the home-screen widget, the share sheet, or the + button
> anywhere in the app. Decide what it means later.
>
> **Decide what it means.** When you have a minute, give each thought a
> place: today, this week, a date, someone else's job, or "someday" if it's
> just an idea. A few taps, no forms.
>
> **Keep today small.** Pick at most three must-dos. Everything else stays
> visible but quiet. Missed something? It's carried over, never shown in red,
> never held against you.
>
> **Review once a week.** A guided 15-minute review empties your head,
> clears the inbox and looks at the week ahead, so nothing slips.
>
> **When it's all too much.** The five-minute reset narrows everything down
> to one thing, and shows you only that.
>
> **Also:** reminders that respect quiet hours, repeating items that never
> pile up, steps and a "done means…" for anything vague, search, a Today
> widget with one-tap done, dark mode, and support for TalkBack and large text.
>
> **Private by design.** No account. No ads. No analytics. Clearing has no
> internet access at all: everything stays on your phone, and you can export
> a backup whenever you like.
>
> Clearing is free and open source.

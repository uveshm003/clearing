# End-of-beta survey

Sent outside the app (e.g. a form) after 2–4 weeks. Opt-in, anonymous. The
first three questions map to the PRD §11 success criteria.

1. **Compared with before the beta, how often did you forget something you'd
   committed to?** Much less · A bit less · About the same · A bit more ·
   Much more
2. **Compared with before, how much time did you spend deciding what to do
   next?** Much less · A bit less · About the same · A bit more · Much more
3. **Did you turn notifications off, or routinely dismiss them without
   reading?** No · Sometimes · Yes, I turned them off · Yes, I dismiss them
   - If yes: why?
4. How many days a week did you open Clearing? 0–1 · 2–3 · 4–5 · 6–7
5. Did you do a weekly review? Never · Once · Most weeks · Every week
   - Roughly how long did it take?
6. Which did you use? Quick-capture widget · Today widget · Share to
   Clearing · Repeating items · Overwhelm reset · Search · Backup
7. Did anything make you feel guilty or nagged? Where?
8. Which words or screens were confusing?
9. What's the one thing you'd change?
10. Would you keep using Clearing? Definitely · Probably · Not sure ·
    Probably not · No
11. Phone model and Android version (optional, helps with reminder issues).

**Targets:** a majority report improvement on Q1 and Q2; fewer than 1 in 5
answer "turned them off" or "dismiss them" on Q3.
